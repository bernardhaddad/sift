How a trace works

  1. Take the token's 20 largest accounts and their owners, and skip pools and programs.
  2. For every holder wallet, find who paid for its very first transaction, up to two hops back, and who sent it its first tokens.
  3. Classify each of those funding wallets: exchanges and programs are public and link nobody, while private wallets do.
  4. Holders that lead back to the same private wallet form a linked group. Its share of supply is what one actor most likely controls.

The verdicts

    Limits, honestly

    • Only the 20 largest accounts are sampled, and wallets with over 1,000 transactions are not traced. A clean result is not a safety guarantee.
    • A linked group is strong on-chain evidence that one actor holds several wallets. It is not proof of what they will do.
    • The same thresholds decide what Sift's own paper books refuse to trade. Results are updated hourly for the tokens the scanner watches; requested traces keep their date.
    • Nothing here is financial advice. Sift never sells, promotes, or calls tokens.
