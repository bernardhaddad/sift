Track record

Does a verdict predict what the price does next? Every token enters this record the first hour it is published, with its verdict frozen, and its price is followed for 7 days.

Wallets behind several tokens

Wallets that funded or supplied top holders of two or more tokens, remembered across every trace since the registry started, including tokens no longer on this page (a wallet seen behind only one token is forgotten after 30 days). Some are shared services (bots, relayers); some are one deployer behind several launches. The full registry is public: radar-wallets.json.

    How a trace works

    1. Take the token's 20 largest accounts and their owners, and skip pools and programs.
    2. For every holder wallet with under 1,000 transactions, find who paid for its very first transaction, up to two hops back. For every holder, find who sent its token account its first tokens.
    3. Classify each of those wallets. Programs and exchange hot wallets (over 3,000 transactions and 250 SOL) are public and link nobody; every other wallet is private.
    4. Holders that lead back to the same private wallet form a linked group. Its share of supply is what one actor most likely controls. Every link is listed with the transaction that proves it, so you can check it yourself on Solscan.
    5. A link through a private wallet with over 3,000 transactions but a small balance (possibly an exchange withdrawal wallet or a bot) still counts for Sift's own trading, but is not enough for a "High insider risk" verdict here. The same goes for a wallet seen behind top holders of several tokens: Sift's trading treats it as a shared service, while this page still shows the group, flagged, because it can also be one deployer behind several tokens.

    The verdicts

      How the track record is kept

      • A token enters the first hour it is published with a price on GeckoTerminal. Its verdict is frozen at that moment; a later re-grade never rewrites the record.
      • Its price is sampled every hour for 7 days: the lowest price, the price 24 to 30 hours after entry and the price 7 days to 7 days 6 hours after entry, each with the hours actually elapsed.
      • Collapsed means the lowest hourly price fell to 20% of the entry price or less (−80%), or the price service reported no price for the token for its last 24 hours (it stopped trading).
      • A verdict's numbers are shown once 10 of its tokens have finished their 7 days. Verdicts are not assigned at random (scanner tokens are established pools; requests and trending tokens skew to new launches, and trending ones enter at peak attention), so a difference between verdicts is evidence, not proof. Prices are sampled hourly, so a crash and recovery inside one hour is not seen. An entry whose prices could not be checked for over 6 hours in a row is not scored. The full record is public: radar-ledger.json.

      Free data

      Everything on this page is published as plain files, updated every hour, free to use with no key. Please credit Sift Insider Radar and link back.

      • radar.json: every token on the page. Per token: mint, name, grade (high, watch, low, incomplete), cluster_pct and clustered_pct (largest and all linked groups, % of supply), unknown_pct, top_pct, traced_at, source, and clusters: each with its holders (wallets, with % of supply) and links (from, to, kind: funded or tokens, and tx, the proving transaction). Also the scorecard and repeat_wallets.
      • radar-ledger.json: the track record. One entry per token: verdict at entry, entry price p0, lowest price, the 24-hour and 7-day samples with hours elapsed, and vanished / void / closed.
      • radar-wallets.json: the funder-wallet registry. Per wallet: every token it was seen behind (first and last seen, verdict) and flags (busy, known).
      • radar-audit.json: the self-audit. Each hour a sample of published links is re-checked against a separate Solana node with independent code: for "funded", that the proof transaction's fee payer is the funder and it is the wallet's first transaction; for "tokens", that the transaction moved tokens under the sender's authority to the holder. Every check is listed with its result.
      • radar-feed.xml: RSS alerts for new High and Watch verdicts and for wallets seen behind a second token. Works in any feed reader, or in a Telegram or Discord RSS bot.

      Limits

      • Besides the tokens Sift's scanner watches and the ones people request, the radar traces up to two trending Solana tokens each hour that no trace covered in the last day.
      • Only the 20 largest accounts are sampled, and wallets with over 1,000 transactions are not traced back to their funder. A clean result is not a safety guarantee.
      • Results are shown while they are current: scanner traces for 24 hours (2 hours if incomplete), requested traces for 7 days, each with its date.
      • A linked group is strong on-chain evidence that one actor holds several wallets. It is not proof of what they will do.
      • The same thresholds decide what Sift's own paper books refuse to trade. Results are updated hourly for the tokens the scanner watches.
      • Nothing here is financial advice. Sift never sells, promotes, or calls tokens.
