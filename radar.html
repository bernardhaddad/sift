    Method

    1. Top holders. The token's 20 largest accounts and their owners. Pools, programs and program addresses are set aside; they are not people.
    2. Funding. For each holder, who paid for its first transaction (two hops back) and who sent it its first tokens. Exchanges link nobody.
    3. Groups. Holders that lead to the same private wallet form a linked group, and each link is listed with its transaction.
    The verdicts
      Weaker evidence and how it is flagged
      • A link through a private wallet with over 3,000 transactions but a small balance (possibly an exchange withdrawal wallet or a bot) still counts for Sift's own trading, but is not enough for a "High insider risk" verdict here.
      • A wallet seen behind top holders of several tokens is shown, flagged: it can be a shared service such as a bot, or one deployer behind several tokens.
      How the track record is kept
      • A token enters the first hour it is published with a price on GeckoTerminal. Its verdict is frozen at that moment; a later re-grade never rewrites the record.
      • Its price is sampled every hour for 7 days: the lowest price, the price 24 to 30 hours after entry and the price 7 days to 7 days 6 hours after entry, each with the hours actually elapsed.
      • Collapsed means the lowest hourly price fell to 20% of the entry price or less (−80%), or the price service reported no price for the token for its last 24 hours (it stopped trading).
      • A verdict's numbers are shown once 10 of its tokens have finished their 7 days. Verdicts are not assigned at random (scanner tokens are established pools; requests and trending tokens skew to new launches, and trending ones enter at peak attention), so a difference between verdicts is evidence, not proof. Prices are sampled hourly, so a crash and recovery inside one hour is not seen. An entry whose prices could not be checked for over 6 hours in a row is not scored.
      Self-audit

      Each hour a sample of published links is re-checked against a separate Solana node with code that shares nothing with the tracer: for a funding link, that the proof transaction's fee payer is the funder and that it is the wallet's first transaction; for a token link, that the transaction moved tokens under the sender's authority to the holder. A link the node could not answer for is never counted as passed.

      Limits
      • Besides the tokens Sift's scanner watches and the ones people request, the radar traces up to two trending Solana tokens each hour that no trace covered in the last day.
      • Only the 20 largest accounts are sampled, and wallets with over 1,000 transactions are not traced back to their funder. A clean result is not a safety guarantee.
      • Results are shown while they are current: scanner traces for 24 hours (2 hours if incomplete), requested traces for 7 days, each with its date.
      • A linked group is strong on-chain evidence that one actor holds several wallets. It is not proof of what they will do.
      • Nothing here is financial advice. Sift never sells, promotes, or calls tokens.

      Data

      The page is built from these files, updated hourly. Free to use; please link back.

      radar.json
      Every token: verdict, linked supply, holders, and every link with its proof transaction.
      radar-ledger.json
      The track record: verdict at entry, entry price, lowest price, 24-hour and 7-day samples.
      radar-wallets.json
      The wallet memory: every token each funder wallet was seen behind, with flags.
      radar-audit.json
      The self-audit: every re-checked link with its result.
      radar-feed.xml
      RSS alerts for new High and Watch verdicts and repeat wallets. Works in Telegram and Discord RSS bots.
