Solana memecoin pools, rescanned every hour: buy and sell flow, holder clusters, and the paper record of every rule tested on them.
Nothing passes these filters right now. That is usually the right answer: most of the leaderboard is under 24 hours old or already sold off.

Refused by the funding-cluster gate

For each token our paper system considers, the top 20 holders are traced back through who funded them and who sent them tokens. Wallets that share a private funder are one cluster. Tokens where one cluster holds more than of supply, or all clusters together more than , are refused. Public rug-checkers pass most of these.

How to read the table

Quiet dips
Pools 7 – 30 days old with at least $250K liquidity, 24h volume $100K – $1.7M, down 2 – 15% on the day, with a buy ratio between 45% and 62%. The only band in our panel mining whose forward returns cleared a 3% round-trip cost.
Buy ratio
Buys ÷ (buys + sells) by transaction count over 24 hours. Counts are what manufactured demand inflates, so read it next to flow.
Flow
Net dollars (buys minus sells) over the most recent trades we sampled, with the hours that sample covers. Sampled for the pools our paper books watch, not for every row.
Buyers / sellers
Unique addresses on each side over 24 hours. A high buy ratio with few unique buyers is one wallet, not demand.
Cluster
Result of the funding-cluster trace above. "no data" means the trace ran out of time or budget and the paper system treats that as a refusal, not a pass.
Heat
Mentions across the Telegram call channels we watch in the last cycle. Our paper books exit on heat; called tokens lost 19% at three months in the studies we verified.
In panel
Days since the pool first appeared in our hourly scan. Survival is a signal in a market where most launches are gone within a day.
