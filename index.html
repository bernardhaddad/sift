How to read the table
Quiet dips
Pools 7–30 days old with at least $250K liquidity, 24h volume $100K–$1.7M, down 2–15% on the day, with a buy ratio between 45% and 62%. The only band in the panel mining whose forward returns cleared a 3% round-trip cost.
Buy ratio
Buys ÷ (buys + sells) by transaction count over 24 hours. Counts are what manufactured demand inflates, so read it next to flow.
Flow
Net dollars (buys minus sells) over the most recent sampled trades, with the hours that sample covers. Sampled for the pools the paper books watch, not for every row.
Cluster
Result of the funding-cluster trace above. "no data" means the trace ran out of time or budget. The paper system treats that as a refusal.
